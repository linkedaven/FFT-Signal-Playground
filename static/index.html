The spectrum is the single-sided amplitude spectrum, scaled so a sine of amplitude A peaks at A for every window. Peak frequencies use parabolic interpolation between bins. Filters are zero-phase Butterworth-shaped magnitude responses applied in the frequency domain on a zero-padded copy, so the output has no phase shift.
